What it does

A working surface for decisions while the meeting is still happening.

Captures the decision record

Room Clarity watches transcript cues and keeps a visible board of decisions, risks, and action items for the host to confirm.

Gives in-meeting feedback

Built-in decision-support agents flag assumptions, failure modes, and weak arguments while the host still has a chance to ask a better question.

Adapts to your team

Room Clarity is designed to support custom agent skills, so teams can add prompts, review patterns, and decision checks that match how they work.

Shares cleanly

The host can share the app surface in Zoom or open a hard-to-guess dashboard URL for screen sharing and review.

Install

Add Room Clarity to Zoom.

  1. Install or request approval. Open the Room Clarity Zoom Marketplace listing. If your organization requires approval, request it from your Zoom administrator.
  2. Launch it from Zoom Apps. Start a Zoom meeting, open the Apps panel, and choose Room Clarity. The app creates a meeting board for that session.
  3. Share the board with the room. Use the app surface directly or open the dashboard in a browser and screen-share it when the group needs a shared artifact.
  4. Review before relying on outputs. Generated decisions and prompts are assistance only. The host should confirm records before treating them as the meeting outcome.
