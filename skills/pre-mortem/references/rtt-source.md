# RTT Source Notes: Pre-Mortem Analysis

Based on the RTT Pre-Mortem Analysis and Advanced Pre-Mortem methods from the provided RTT materials.

Core goal: understand the ways strategies, plans, and decisions could fail, then take proactive steps to prevent or mitigate failure.

Use after a plan has been formulated but before final approval or execution.

Method summary:

1. Imagine the plan has failed, ideally in a vivid and concrete way.
2. Work backward from the failed state to identify the sequence of events that led there.
3. Identify common or high-consequence points of failure.
4. Modify the plan to avoid those failures or mitigate them if they occur.
5. Identify signposts of failure and assign monitoring or response responsibilities when appropriate.

Meeting adaptation: surface one plausible failure path at the moment the team is converging, and pair it with a mitigation or warning sign.
