# RTT Source Notes: Assumptions Challenge

Based on the RTT Assumptions Challenge method from the provided RTT materials.

Core goal: identify and stress-test the stated and unstated assumptions that underlie a strategy, plan, or decision.

Use when evaluating a strategy, plan, recommendation, or decision.

Method summary:

1. List stated and unstated assumptions behind the plan, strategy, or decision.
2. Identify the most critical assumptions, optionally using likelihood, impact, and ease of addressing.
3. Challenge critical assumptions with questions such as:
   - Is this based on cognitive bias or preconceived notions?
   - Is this based on past experience, and is that experience relevant?
   - What has to happen for this to be true?
   - If this becomes true, does it remain true under all conditions?
   - If this is untrue, how does it alter the plan?
   - How much confidence do we have that this will prove true?
   - How could the plan be altered to strengthen this assumption or mitigate its failure?

Meeting adaptation: surface only the most consequential assumption during live discussion, with evidence and a host-readable question.
