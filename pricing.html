Usage limits

What counts as a meeting analysis hour?

Counts toward your plan

  • Live Zoom transcript analysis
  • Uploaded transcript analysis
  • AI-generated recaps and brief drafts
  • User-requested re-analysis or regeneration

Does not count

  • Viewing existing boards
  • Editing recap items
  • Reading or sharing an existing brief
  • Using synthetic demo data

Limit behavior

  • Current meetings finish even if usage crosses the monthly pool
  • New analyzed meetings may pause until upgrade or reset
  • No automatic overage charges at launch
  • Existing artifacts remain available subject to retention settings

Questions

A few details we expect buyers to ask.

Why charge by meeting analysis hours?

Meeting hours match how people understand the product. They also protect the service from runaway usage without making customers think about tokens, model calls, or transcript chunks.

Will the app stop working mid-meeting?

No. The intended behavior is to let an active meeting finish, then ask the user to renew, contact us, or wait for the next billing period before starting new analyzed meetings.

Is this just meeting transcription?

No. Room Clarity is priced around decision value: captured decisions, visible risks, action ownership, agent prompts, reviewed briefs, and continuity across meetings.
