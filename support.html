Support

Meeting Decision Maker is currently in beta testing.

Getting Help

If the app was shared with you by an organization or test administrator, contact that administrator for support, access questions, and bug reports.

Common Checks

  • Confirm the app is installed from the Zoom Marketplace local test or approved listing.
  • Confirm the app has been authorized with the requested Zoom scopes.
  • Refresh or reopen the Zoom App if the latest version does not appear.
  • Use the app only in meetings where transcript-based assistance is authorized.
